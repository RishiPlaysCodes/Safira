Intelligent Safety Features

Smart Accident Detection

Multi-signal analysis: impact + speed drop + no movement + phone angle. Avoids false alarms from potholes and bumps.

Live Location Sharing

Real-time location sharing with guardians during rides and emergencies. Shareable tracking link.

Guardian Auto-Alert

SMS + call + push notification to parents/guardians. 20-second countdown with "I Am Safe" cancel option.

Zone-Based Speed Limits

Auto-adjusts speed limits near schools, hospitals, and danger zones using GPS and map data.

Helmet Detection (AI)

Camera-based helmet detection using TFLite models. Instant guardian notification if helmet not worn.

Weekly Safety Reports

Comprehensive weekly reports sent to guardians: overspeed, violations, helmet issues, risk analysis.
